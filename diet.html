Practical Indian Nutrition Strategies:

1. Boost Lentil Bioavailability: Combining dal with whole wheat roti or brown rice creates a complete amino acid profile with all 9 essential amino acids.

2. The Magic of Dahi & Chaas: Probiotics in fresh homemade curd improve gut microbiota, accelerating nutrient absorption and reducing workout inflammation.

3. Soya Chunks & Paneer Synergy: Soya chunks offer over 52g protein per 100g dry weight, making them one of the most cost-effective muscle builders in an Indian diet.

4. Spice Wisdom: Turmeric (curcumin), cumin (jeera), and black pepper not only add flavor but aid gastric motility and insulin sensitivity.
