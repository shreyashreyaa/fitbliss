Calculation Reference:
  • BMI: Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²
  • BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor): Men = 10×W + 6.25×H - 5×Age + 5; Women = 10×W + 6.25×H - 5×Age - 161
  • TDEE: BMR × Activity Multiplier (1.2 to 1.9)
  • Target: Loss = TDEE - 500 kcal; Gain = TDEE + 300 kcal
Medical & Clinical Notice: These health calculations are mathematical estimates derived from standard peer-reviewed equations (including Mifflin-St Jeor and WHO BMI classifications). They are intended exclusively for general wellness tracking and education, not clinical diagnostics, medical advice, or prescribing eating plans. Always consult your primary physician, endocrinologist, or registered sports dietitian before undertaking radical dietary adjustments or intensive resistance training.
